Remote Support

Remote support is the quickest way to diagnose PLC, VFD, HMI, and comms issues — especially intermittent faults and logic/parameter problems.

Request Remote Support Plant Down?

Best for

  • PLC troubleshooting & logic edits
  • Drive faults, parameter verification, control strategy
  • HMI alarms, tag mapping, and visibility issues
  • EtherNet/IP drops and device timeouts
  • Repeat failures you want to stop permanently

How a remote session works

  1. Submit the request with symptoms and contact info.
  2. Rapid triage to identify what stopped and what changed.
  3. Connect securely using your plant-approved method (VPN / remote desktop / on-site tech).
  4. Fix + verify — changes are documented and validated.
  5. Handoff with notes so maintenance can repeat the recovery steps.

What we need

  • PLC/HMI/drive platform (if known)
  • Fault codes and screenshots
  • Someone on-site to confirm field conditions
  • Any recent changes (hardware, wiring, logic, recipes)

If you can’t share screenshots through the form, submit anyway and reply to the confirmation email.

Remote vs on-site

Remote gets you diagnosis and many fixes fast. On-site is better when the issue is physical: wiring, sensors, safety circuits, motors, mechanical binding, or panel work.

If the situation needs hands-on work, we’ll tell you quickly — no wasted time.
