Condition Monitoring

Early warning that reduces downtime without adding noise.

Catch developing failures early, reduce repeat downtime, and document chronic issues with actionable alerts.

Manager summary: Early warning + clear context + documented patterns. Less emergency work and fewer repeat stops.

PLANT DOWN

Immediate response

Production is impacted now. Alerts prioritize urgency, location, and first checks to restore operation fast.

  • High urgency
  • Restore operation first
  • Root-cause follow-up documented
TRENDING ISSUE

Early warning

A developing condition or recurring pattern likely to become downtime if ignored. Plan the fix before it stops production.

  • Lower urgency, higher payoff
  • Plan parts and schedule
  • Stops repeat failures

What gets monitored

  • Motor and VFD faults, warnings, and trending conditions
  • Safety interruptions and nuisance trips
  • Sensor failures, bad inputs, intermittent signals
  • Cycle counts, run hours, stop frequency
  • Recurring faults that do not always hard-stop the line

Focus is on failure patterns and usable context.

What alerts include

  • Asset or line affected
  • What changed versus normal operation
  • Whether it is new, trending, or recurring
  • Details that guide the first check

If it alerts, it is worth looking at.

What managers get

  • Fewer surprise breakdowns
  • Faster troubleshooting
  • Clear documentation of chronic issues
  • Better justification for parts, PM time, and upgrades
  • Better handoff between shifts
