What makes this different

Most condition monitoring fails because it becomes a dashboard nobody trusts. We keep it plant-ready: start with what you already track, prove value fast, then expand only where it pays off.

Start with your CMMS + parts room

Repeat failures and emergency part pulls are early warnings. We translate that noise into a short list of assets and failure modes worth monitoring.

  • Repeat bearing/motor/gearbox replacements on the same assets
  • Shortening time-between-failure
  • Downtime patterns tied to load, product, washdown, or shift
  • Long lead-time parts that extend downtime

Add sensors where it pays off

We don’t blanket the plant with sensors. We target the few assets that cause the most pain and give you alarms and trends that your team will actually use.

  • High-impact bottlenecks
  • Hard-to-access drives/gearboxes
  • Assets with expensive collateral damage
  • Equipment where “failure happens fast”

What we monitor

Pick the right signals for the failure mode. Don’t guess.

Vibration

Bearings, gearboxes, fans, pumps, conveyors — misalignment, imbalance, looseness, wear.

Temperature / thermal

Motors, cabinets, drives, friction points — overheating, abnormal patterns, weak connections.

Electrical signatures

Load trends, imbalance, overheating under low speed, abnormal torque/current behavior.

Ultrasonic / acoustic

Air leaks, valve leakage, cavitation, friction changes that vibration may miss.

Lubrication / oil condition

Contamination and breakdown trends so changes happen at the right time.

Process trends

Pressure/flow/speed/output/downtime patterns tied to maintenance history.

FAQ

Quick answers to the stuff everyone asks.

Do we need new sensors to get value?
Not always. We start with CMMS/parts usage and downtime notes first. Sensors come second — only where it makes financial sense.
Will this work on older equipment?
Yes. That’s the point. We’re not waiting for a full modernization to reduce downtime. Monitoring and trend-based decisions can be layered onto legacy systems.
How do you prevent “ignored dashboard syndrome”?
We keep outputs simple: a short list of assets, trend views that answer “is it getting worse,” and clear actions your team can execute. If it can’t drive a decision, it doesn’t belong.
What do you need from us to start?
CMMS exports (even partial), parts usage history, downtime notes, and a list of your biggest pain assets. If you don’t have it clean, we can still start with what you’ve got.
