Start with the parts room

Your storeroom and CMMS already contain early-warning signals — repeat failures, emergency part pulls, and downtime patterns.

What we look for

  • Repeated bearing, seal, motor, or gearbox replacements on the same assets
  • Shortening time-between-failure on specific components
  • High-usage or constantly missing spares
  • Emergency pulls vs planned work
  • Long lead-time items that consistently extend downtime

Why it works

Parts usage trends often show deterioration weeks or months before a breakdown. We correlate usage with runtime, operating conditions, and failure history to turn “repeat problems” into planned work — and smarter stocking.

Fast time to value

Starting with existing data means you get results fast — without waiting months to “train” a model or buying sensors for every piece of equipment.

Condition monitoring where it pays off

We add targeted sensing on high-impact assets — not a sensor blanket that turns into another ignored dashboard.

Vibration

Bearings, gearboxes, conveyors, fans, pumps — misalignment, imbalance, wear, looseness.

Temperature & thermal

Friction points, motors, electrical cabinets, drives — overheating and abnormal heat patterns.

Electrical / motor health

Electrical imbalance, insulation degradation, rotor/stator issues, abnormal load signatures.

Ultrasonic / acoustic

Air leaks, valve leakage, cavitation, early friction changes that vibration may miss.

Oil & lubrication condition

Contamination and breakdown trends so changes happen at the right time — not too early or too late.

Process & performance trends

Pressure/flow/speed/output/downtime patterns tied back to maintenance and parts history.
