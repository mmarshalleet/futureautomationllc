Plant Down Support

When it’s down, we move fast: triage across PLCs, HMIs, VFDs, sensors, safety, and panels.

  • Lock the fault: power, comms, interlocks, and I/O — verified, not guessed.
  • Isolate the root cause and get you running without “try it and see.”
  • Patch it clean and document the fix so it doesn’t come back next shift.

Repeat‑Stop Elimination

Stop the same nuisance fault from stealing another shift. We find the pattern and kill it.

  • Build a stop-history “kill list” from alarms, trips, and operator notes.
  • Instrument the problem: trending, timestamps, and interlock truth tables.
  • Implement the permanent fix: wiring, logic, parameter, or mechanical interface.
