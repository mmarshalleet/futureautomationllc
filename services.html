Services

When production is unstable or down, you need fast diagnosis and clean fixes. We support plants locally and remotely with practical controls troubleshooting.

PLC Troubleshooting & Logic Changes

Nuisance faults, intermittent stops, and “it only does it sometimes” issues. We diagnose root cause, correct logic safely, and document changes.

  • Fault tracing & I/O validation
  • Logic cleanup and safe edits
  • Startup/sequence issues
  • Legacy support & migrations

VFD & Motor Control

Trips, ground faults, comm errors, parameter mismatches, and speed/torque issues. We verify setup, wiring assumptions, feedback, and control strategy.

  • Drive fault diagnosis & recovery
  • Parameter verification
  • PLC/drive integration
  • Interlocks & permissives

HMI & Communications

When operators can’t trust the screen, downtime gets expensive. We stabilize comms and make the HMI reflect reality.

  • EtherNet/IP device drops & timeouts
  • Alarm accuracy & usability
  • Tag mapping and data integrity
  • Network sanity checks

Emergency Plant-Down

Production stopped or at risk. Immediate triage, rapid diagnosis, and clear next steps. Remote first when possible, on-site when required.
