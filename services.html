What to send for faster help

  • Machine/line name + plant/location
  • Symptoms and when it started
  • Fault codes / screenshots
  • What changed recently (maintenance, parts swapped, parameter edits)
  • Photos of panel/wiring if relevant

What we don’t do

  • We don’t guess or shotgun parts.
  • We don’t sell hardware to justify a service call.
  • We don’t blur mechanical issues into “controls problems.”
  • We don’t leave without explaining what failed and why.
