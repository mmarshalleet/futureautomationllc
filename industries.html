Industries & Environments

Controls problems look different depending on the plant. We’re comfortable in high-uptime environments where downtime costs money fast.

Food & Poultry Processing

Wet environments, aggressive washdown schedules, and nonstop production demands. We focus on fast troubleshooting and repeat-failure prevention.

  • Conveyance, packaging, and processing lines
  • Intermittent sensor/IO faults
  • Drive trips and comm drops

Manufacturing & Packaging

Start/stop sequences, safety interlocks, and multi-device coordination. We keep controls stable and operators informed.

  • Sequencing and nuisance downtime
  • HMI alarms and visibility
  • Device networking and stability

Utilities & Support Systems

Systems that can’t quietly fail: pumps, blowers, fans, wastewater, and plant utilities. We prioritize reliability and clear alarms.

  • Pump/VFD control and protection
  • Fail-safe logic and permissives
  • Operator clarity

General Industrial

If it runs on a PLC and it’s causing downtime, we can usually help. Remote support is often the fastest path to diagnosis.
