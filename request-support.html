Request Support

Plant down, nuisance trips, VFD faults, weird comms issues—send the details and we’ll get eyes on it fast.

What to include

  • Line / area + what stopped
  • PLC / HMI / drive model (if you know it)
  • Alarms, screenshots, fault codes
  • Best call-back number + site time zone
Heads up: If you can’t text photos through the form, submit the request anyway—reply to the confirmation email with attachments.
